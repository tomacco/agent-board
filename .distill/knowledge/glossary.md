# Glossary

- **Hands off**: switch that forbids agents to speak, play sounds or drive mouse and keyboard; the file `~/.agent-board/hands-off`. evidence: README.md
- **Pill**: the single menu bar status item (Clawd, cup, rack). evidence: README.md
- **Clawd**: the pixel mascot in the pill. evidence: Bar.swift#Art
- **boardd**: the enforcer LaunchAgent that kills blocked processes while hands off is on. evidence: bin/boardd
